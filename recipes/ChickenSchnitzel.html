Chicken Schnitzel

Chicken Schnitzel is a popular European dish made from thin, tender chicken breasts coated in seasoned flour, beaten egg, and crispy breadcrumbs. The chicken is fried until golden brown and crunchy on the outside while remaining juicy and tender inside.

Traditionally served with lemon wedges and a side of potatoes or fresh salad, schnitzel is a simple yet incredibly satisfying meal. Its crispy golden coating and tender chicken make it a favorite for both family dinners and casual meals.

Ingredients:

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs (preferably panko)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 teaspoon paprika (optional)
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Lemon wedges for serving
