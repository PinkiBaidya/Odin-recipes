Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian comfort food made with layers of tender pasta sheets, rich tomato-based meat sauce, creamy béchamel or ricotta filling, and plenty of melted cheese. Each layer comes together in the oven to create a hearty, flavorful dish with a beautifully golden, bubbling top.

This delicious lasagna is perfect for family dinners, gatherings, or whenever you want a warm and satisfying homemade meal. The combination of savory sauce, creamy cheese, and perfectly baked pasta makes every bite rich, comforting, and full of flavor.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)
